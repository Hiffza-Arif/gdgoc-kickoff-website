About GDGOC

Google Developer Groups (GDG) are communities where developers and students come together to learn about Google technologies, improve their technical skills, share knowledge, and collaborate on innovative projects. GDG encourages learning, creativity, and growth in the tech community.

GDGOC Teams

Web Team

The Web Team focuses on web development, creating websites and web applications using various technologies. They collaborate on projects, share knowledge, and contribute to the GDGOC community by building innovative web solutions.

App Team

The App Team specializes in app development, working on Android and iOS platforms. They explore mobile technologies, develop apps, and engage with the community to enhance their skills and contribute to mobile innovation.

Media Team

The Media Team is responsible for creating and managing content for the GDGOC community. They handle social media, photography, videography, and other forms of communication to promote events and share knowledge.

PR Team

The PR Team manages public relations and outreach for GDGOC. They work on building relationships with external organizations, promoting events, and ensuring effective communication with the community and stakeholders.

AI/ML Team

The AI/ML Team focuses on artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies. They explore algorithms, data analysis, and AI applications, contributing to the GDGOC community by sharing insights and developing innovative AI/ML solutions.

Management Team

A dedicated team that leads with teamwork, creativity, and commitment to make every GDGOC event successful.

Women in Tech Team

Empowering women to explore technology, build confidence, and become future leaders in the digital world.
